Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy covers the Taro & Yuzu YouTube channel and the "Taro & Yuzu uploader", a private tool operated by Abdelrahman Al-Omari (ARO Solutions) that uses YouTube API Services to upload the show's own videos to the Taro & Yuzu channel.

Who uses the uploader

Only the channel owner. The uploader is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What the uploader accesses and stores

  • With the owner's consent, it uses Google OAuth to obtain permission (youtube.upload and youtube scopes) to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags, translations, privacy and made-for-kids status on the owner's own channel.
  • It stores a single OAuth refresh token, encrypted as a secret in the owner's private GitHub repository, so uploads can run on a schedule. It stores the IDs and titles of videos it has uploaded in that repository.
  • It does not collect, read, store or share any information about viewers, other YouTube users, comments, or any third party, and it does not use cookies or analytics.

Sharing

No data obtained through YouTube API Services is sold or shared with anyone. Videos are sent only to YouTube.

Children

The channel's videos are made for kids and are marked as such on YouTube. Neither the channel owner nor the uploader collects personal information from children. YouTube's own handling of viewer data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

YouTube and Google

By using this uploader, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking it deletes nothing from YouTube; the uploader simply stops working. Stored tokens are deleted from the repository on request or when the uploader is retired.
